Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DramaPop (“we”, “us”) handles information in the DramaPop mobile application (the “App”). By using the App you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Anonymous account identifier. We create an anonymous user ID (via Firebase Authentication) so we can save your unlocks, coins, and render requests. We do not require your name, email, or phone number to use the App.
  • Content you submit. When you use the Create feature, we process the text prompts/loglines you enter in order to generate a video for you.
  • Purchase information. When you buy coins or a subscription, the transaction is handled by Apple and our payments provider (RevenueCat). We receive purchase status and receipts, not your full payment card details.
  • Usage & device data. Basic diagnostics and usage events (e.g. app version, device type, crashes, feature interactions) to operate and improve the App.

2. AI-generated content

Videos and text in the App are generated by artificial intelligence and are fictional. They may be inaccurate or unrealistic and are provided for entertainment only.

3. How we use information

  • Provide core features (playback, unlocks, coins, AI video generation).
  • Process purchases and prevent fraud/abuse of free credits.
  • Maintain, secure, debug, and improve the App.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Service providers

We share limited data with processors who run the App on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database) — privacy.
  • Cloudflare Stream (video hosting/delivery) — privacy.
  • RevenueCat (purchase management) — privacy.
  • Apple (App Store, payments) — privacy.

5. What we do NOT do

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

6. Data retention

We keep data for as long as needed to provide the App and for legitimate business or legal purposes. You can request deletion of your data at any time (see Contact).

7. Children

The App is rated 17+ and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, or object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, email support@playbops.com. Because accounts are anonymous, please contact us from the device in question so we can identify your data.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date.
